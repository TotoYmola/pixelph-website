CITY STANDARDS

Roleplay first. Respect always.

This page is the public rules summary. Staff decisions and the full rulebook apply to all players, and rules may be updated as the city evolves.

Read Full RulebookAsk in Discord

Roleplay Integrity

  • Stay in character during active scenes.
  • Do not metagame, powergame, or force outcomes without giving others a fair chance to respond.
  • Value your character's life and treat serious situations with appropriate weight.

Fair Play

  • No cheats, exploits, injected menus, unfair visual/audio advantages, or intentional bug abuse.
  • Report serious bugs instead of benefiting from them.
  • Do not evade punishments or use alternate accounts to bypass staff action.

Community Conduct

  • No harassment, hate speech, targeted abuse, or behavior intended to ruin another player's experience.
  • Keep disputes out of active RP and use the proper support channels.
  • Follow staff instructions while a case is being handled.

Character Standards

  • Create believable names, motivations, and behavior appropriate for serious RP.
  • Keep knowledge separated between different characters.
  • Do not use a character primarily to troll, bait, or disrupt scenes.
