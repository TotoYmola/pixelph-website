OFFICIAL CITY CODE

PixelPH Serious Roleplay Rules

Believable characters. Meaningful consequences. Long-term stories. By entering PixelPH, you accept responsibility for knowing and following these standards.

Character before Player • Story before Victory • Consequences before Convenience
01 — PixelPH Community Code

Respect is the baseline of PixelPH in both city and community spaces. Harassment, discrimination, targeted personal attacks, threats, and slurs are prohibited. Keep character conflict IC; do not carry hostility into Discord, private messages, streams, or real life. Use proper Discord channels and never expose information that can create unfair IC knowledge. Reports, appeals, and disputes belong in the official support system.

02 — City Immersion Standard

While inside the city, character comes before player. Communication that affects active RP must use available in-city systems; third-party calls or messages cannot coordinate IC actions. A working microphone is required. Character names must be believable. Stay in character during active scenes and, when reasonably possible, finish the scene before reporting a rule concern.

03 — Character Knowledge Standard

Your character may act only on information legitimately learned through PixelPH roleplay. Streams, VODs, Discord, private messages, another character’s knowledge, police or EMS information learned on another character, and OOC conversations cannot become IC knowledge. Each character has separate knowledge and relationships. A concealed character requires reasonable IC grounds to identify.

04 — Realistic Action Standard

Do not force outcomes through mechanics, commands, animations, properties, or unrealistic actions. Examples include entering property to escape an active threat or pursuit, abusing carry/escort/seat mechanics, movement abuse for unnatural speed, or using /me to declare an outcome rather than an attempt. /me describes an action or condition; it is not a magic command.

05 — Fair Play & Integrity

Cheats, injected menus, unauthorized software, exploitative visual/audio modifications, duplication, bug abuse, economy manipulation, and unintended mechanics used for advantage are prohibited. Alternate characters cannot transfer money, property, items, criminal information, or assets back to related characters. Unauthorized real-money trading of in-game assets is prohibited. Report impactful exploits instead of continuing to use them.

06 — Scene Initiation Standard

Violence requires context. Lethal escalation needs meaningful interaction, established conflict, an immediate credible threat, or an existing storyline. Attacking or killing without sufficient RP context is unprovoked violence (RDM). Deliberately using a vehicle as a weapon without valid RP justification is vehicle assault (VDM). A rushed one-liner does not automatically justify instant gunfire. Give the other party a reasonable chance to understand the scene when circumstances allow.

07 — Life Preservation Standard

Treat your character’s life as valuable. When facing an obvious lethal disadvantage, behave as though your life is genuinely at risk. Reasonable escape or defense is allowed when a real opportunity exists, but provoking lethal situations or charging armed opponents unrealistically may be Failure to Value Life. Winning is never more important than believable survival.

08 — Roleplay Before Gunplay

Conflict should create stories. Negotiation, intimidation, deception, pursuit, investigation, retaliation, and diplomacy should have room before gunfire. Do not repeatedly hunt or kill a character simply because mechanics allow it. Looting a downed player requires RP justification; do not empty inventories of irrelevant items. Extreme actions such as executions, ocean dumping, or needless vehicle destruction require proportional storyline reasons.

09 — Memory & Recovery Protocol

When a character respawns or is forced into a new-life state after incapacitation, they lose direct memory of the immediate circumstances surrounding that death, including who killed them, the exact location, and the final events of the scene. They cannot immediately return to the active scene. A character properly revived by EMS may retain memory subject to the circumstances. Roleplay confusion, trauma, injuries, and memory gaps naturally.

10 — Injury & Medical Roleplay

Injuries have consequences. A character who has been shot, stabbed, badly struck, or treated for serious trauma should not immediately sprint, fight, or seek revenge as though nothing happened. Respond appropriately to EMS medical interactions and /me examinations. Unconscious players must not provide tactical information. Do not trash-talk incapacitated players who cannot meaningfully participate.

11 — Emergency Services Protocol

Respect EMS and police scenes. If you request EMS and responders arrive, allow medical RP unless the scenario reasonably changes. Targeting or kidnapping EMS requires legitimate buildup. Cop baiting—meaningless acts done solely to force police into a chase or gunfight—is prohibited. Robbing, kidnapping, hunting, or executing officers requires meaningful circumstances and escalation. Escaping police does not justify returning only to shoot responders.

12 — Whitelisted Employment Integrity

Government and whitelisted jobs carry a higher standard. On-duty behavior, clothing, and conduct should reasonably fit the profession. Privileged job mechanics, confidential information, licenses, secured access, government equipment, or internal systems cannot be intentionally used to benefit criminals unless a specifically approved PixelPH storyline or system permits it. Serious abuse may lead to removal from the profession and administrative sanctions.

13 — Criminal Scene Framework

Standard PvP-oriented criminal scenes are limited to four (4) active criminal participants unless a specific activity or approved scenario explicitly sets another limit. The count includes direct participants, lookouts, hackers, getaway drivers, radio intelligence, and anyone actively assisting. No participant swapping after the scene begins. Uninvolved friends cannot suddenly enter as backup. Kidnapping and robbery require proper interaction and reasonable motivation. Fake friend-hostages used only to manipulate mechanics or negotiation are prohibited.

14 — Heist Conduct

Heists are progression content, not repeatable PvP arenas. Follow the participant limit and requirements of the specific activity. Once police engagement begins, do not add outside participants or rotate members. Do not camp known heist locations simply to ambush whoever activates them, and do not intercept another group’s active scripted heist unless that activity explicitly supports interception. Staff may require POV footage for serious disputes. Reputation, clues, and progression are intended to reward long-term discovery.

15 — Organization Framework

Organizations exist to create persistent stories, not permanent deathmatch teams. New organizations undergo a 30-day establishment period during which staff may evaluate activity, leadership, member conduct, RP quality, and contribution. Leaders are responsible for the culture they create, and coordinated/repeated violations can affect the organization as well as individuals. Contacts, suppliers, alliances, enemies, and reputation must develop through RP. Real-world criminal, extremist, or militia branding may not be recreated as PixelPH organizations.

16 — Organization Identity

Organizations should maintain a recognizable identity during declared conflicts through approved colors, clothing, masks, vehicles, symbols, or other representation. Representation should clarify conflict rather than deliberately confuse identification. Outsiders cannot be used as hidden extra members. Membership should develop through RP rather than instant OOC recruitment solely for manpower.

17 — Territory Code

Territory represents influence and responsibility, not automatic KOS. Sprays or markers must be placed reasonably and develop from an organization’s established area. Do not use impossible locations or unintended mechanics to place them. Restricted government locations cannot be claimed unless explicitly supported. Entering territory can create RP risk, but conflict still requires context and appropriate escalation.

18 — Organization Conflict Protocol

Gang wars require story, communication, clear participants, and an endpoint. Petty disputes should not instantly become wars. No endless conflict designed to farm kills, and no importing inactive, newly recruited, or unrelated manpower because one side is losing. KOS exists only when explicitly established under an approved conflict framework. After major engagements, allow reasonable recovery; hospital camping, immediate repeated re-engagement, humiliating downed bodies, and OOC hostility are prohibited.

19 — Alliances & Third-Party Involvement

Alliances require genuine RP development and cannot be created overnight solely to add shooters. An alliance does not automatically grant permission to enter another group’s active conflict. Players and organizations must not insert themselves into scenes they are not part of merely to change the outcome. Territory-specific third-party situations are judged by scene context and applicable organization agreements.

20 — Property & Defensive RP

Entering private property without permission carries IC risk. Owners may use reasonable force to defend themselves, occupants, and property against actual threats or unlawful intrusion, but ownership is not permission for random killing. Police entry should have an appropriate IC legal or emergency basis. Properties cannot be used as mechanic-based safe zones during active pursuit.

21 — Props & World Interaction

Furniture, deployable objects, pets, and world systems exist to support RP. Do not use props to block roads, doors, interaction points, bullets, players, or emergency access for mechanical advantage, and do not create impossible fortifications. Pets cannot be randomly stolen or harmed without meaningful context. World mechanics should support the scene rather than replace roleplay.

22 — Scene Protection Rule

Do not camp hospitals, spawn areas, illegal locations, drug locations, properties, or heist areas solely to repeatedly rob, kill, arrest, or ambush players without RP development. Do not repeatedly target the same character in a short period because you know where they will be. After major violence, allow reasonable recovery. Systematic scene farming may be treated as FailRP even when one isolated action appears technically allowed.

23 — Server Restart Protocol

A server restart does not pause roleplay. Do not begin major illegal activity when there is clearly insufficient time to complete it. Standard illegal activity is restricted during the 30 minutes immediately before and after a scheduled restart unless a specific automated activity is explicitly exempted. Restart timing cannot be exploited to escape police, avoid consequences, force scenes to end, rob vulnerable players, or bypass interaction. After an unexpected interruption, make reasonable efforts to continue the scene.

24 — Failure of Roleplay (FailRP)

FailRP covers behavior that significantly breaks believable interaction even when no single named rule fits perfectly. Examples include tactical coordination while unconscious, ignoring major injuries, mechanic-based scene escapes, extreme escalation over trivial reasons, random looting, repeated killing without story progression, using OOC information IC, or refusing reasonable interaction solely for mechanical advantage. Staff evaluates context, intent, pattern, and impact.

25 — Streaming & Content Conduct

Streaming PixelPH is welcome but grants no special privilege. Stream information cannot become IC knowledge. Do not stream-snipe, backseat active scenes, intentionally expose hidden story information for audience interference, or turn IC rivalries into OOC attacks on creators. Private staff cases, tickets, and sanctions must not become harassment campaigns.

26 — Support & Evidence Policy

When reasonably possible, finish the active scene first and report afterward through the official ticket system. Provide complete context rather than only a clip that favors one side. Evidence may include clips, screenshots, VODs, timestamps, and player IDs. Do not duplicate tickets or pressure staff privately. Ticket contents and another player’s sanctions are confidential unless staff states otherwise.

27 — PixelPH Enforcement Principle

No rulebook can describe every possible situation. A technical loophole does not automatically make an action acceptable. Staff may consider whether an action was realistic, created meaningful RP, gave others a fair chance to participate, abused mechanics, or existed mainly to win. Enforcement exists to protect the quality and continuity of Serious Roleplay.

The PixelPH Standard: You do not need to win every scene, escape every arrest, survive every conflict, or become rich immediately. A loss today can create the story that defines your character tomorrow. Build a character. Create consequences. Leave stories behind.
