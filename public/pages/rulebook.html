Community Conduct

Behavior expected from every member across the city and official community spaces.

Respect & Community Behavior

You must respect one another in and out of the server.

  • Any form of harassment or disrespect will result in consequences.
  • Derogatory remarks directed to others regarding race, gender, religion,

disabilities or sexual preference will not be tolerated.

  • Use proper channels for its purposes. #Media for Pictures/Videos.

#CommunityChat for engaging in a topic discussed but limited to In-Game Information that may lead to spoiling and meta gaming.

  • Do not use this community to advertise. The only exception is to promote your

stream in the proper channels in discord (#streamers-chat)

  • We appreciate all that care about the community and want to help out but

please leave it to our administrators to deal with admin situations.

  • Please have appropriate names. Steam or discord names that are deemed

offensive will receive one warning/kick. If you continue to join after being warned/kicked with the offensive name, a removal will be considered.

*If a problem needs to be resolved by staff, always file a ticket.*

Creator & Streaming Guidelines

Watch what you say.

  • Sharing your opinion is a natural part of streaming. In fact, viewers like it when

you voice your opinion. However, out-of-character rants can be off-putting and even offensive to some, so be sensitive about who you’re speaking to and what your goals are for your live stream while playing inside the server.

  • At the end of the day, just remember to be polite. If something feels

unnecessary or uncalled for, then think about whether you should even say it. Don’t mention anything for the sake of going viral or raising a few eyebrows.

  • Live streaming is no doubt a great medium. But like any other platform or tool, it

needs to be used wisely and carefully. It isn’t something to be abused or taken advantage of just to gain a few clicks—and that goes for both viewers and hosts to other role-players and its community.

Respect the streamer and the community.

  • Keep it civil: Do not make personal attacks on other people. If you need to

criticize another player’s argument, do so without attacking them as a person.

  • Do not use bigoted language, including slurs which degrade another person or

community based on gender, race, sexual orientation, nationality, religion etc.

  • Do not spoil each other’s storyline.
  • Backseat Gaming is when you are telling the person how to play the game or

spoiling the game as well. Don’t backseat a streamer unless they asked for it.

  • Do not discuss players' ban or ban reasoning at any point.
  • Just support each other’s stream.

Staff Interaction Standard

  • Support-Ticket is there for any questions, concerns, and reports etc.
  • The staff is there to clarify any concern or report that you have.
  • Just because you created a ticket doesn’t mean you’re being

“iyakin.”

In certain instances, we will take action if such behavior significantly impacts the players within the city.

Ultimately, it is essential for everyone to coexist harmoniously and remember that this is a role-playing environment, not a competitive game like Counter-Strike, Valorant, or your high school days. Keep disputes mature and protect the roleplay experience instead of turning scenes into OOC negativity.

Gameplay Integrity

Fair-play standards covering client modifications, exploits, and outside transactions.

Client Modification Policy

  • Use of graphics mods is allowed however, players using excessive mods will

face a warning or an immediate ban. This includes sound effects on footsteps, kill effects, no night, clear water, no props, no bush etc.

  • No unrealistic mods as well. (Ex: Confetti headshot, other kill/blood effects, etc.)
  • No Stretched Resolution to benefit the game models, hitbox appear wider on

the screen, and can make aiming a bit easier. It can also lock onto distant targets much more easily.

PS. Keep everything natural as much as possible.

Game Integrity & Exploit Policy

  • Using any third-party software to gain an advantage from anyone.
  • Using mods to gain advantage is NOT allowed.
Ex. Mods that make you see clearly underwater, bushes, loud footsteps etc
  • Exploiting Game Bug
  • Duplicating items
  • Using game-features in unintended ways. (E.g Carrying a body to drop them

through walls and doors. Animation canceling of 2 person animations like tackle or headlock, especially during cops and robbers situations.)

  • Transferring assets/items from main character to second character or any

related character to gain assets.

Multi-Character Exploit = PERMANENT BAN

  • Ex. Creating alternate characters and transferring their money/assets to

someone then having it transferred back to your main character or second character.

Unauthorized Real-Money Transactions

  • Trading of Real Money to in-game items or tax assets may lead both parties

subject to Permanent Ban with NO BAN APPEAL.

Life Preservation Standard

Value your life above all else.

  • Always make reasonable attempts to preserve your life. When threatened with

a firearm, you need to comply.

  • Fists, blunt or bladed weapons or tasers are an exception and you are allowed

to retaliate.

  • You can defend yourself or escape IF the opportunity arises.
  • Your first action in any disagreement should always be to try to roleplay it out.
  • Do not jump straight to gunplay or getting into physical altercation.
  • You are not allowed to shoot someone without properly initiating it. Give them

warnings or a chance to walk away from the current scenario.

  • Once you kill a player during a verbal confrontation you are not allowed to

revive them and ask more questions.

  • Strictly NO SHOOT-FIRST, EXPLAIN-LATER ROLEPLAY
  • If you no longer care for your life, let everyone involved in the current scenario

know your backstory on why so. This could lead to PERMANENT DEATH.

Fear & Survival Response

  • Not realistically fearing for your life when you are threatened with weapons or

severe harm from other means.

  • Having spontaneous shoot outs at PD or Hospital is not allowed.
  • You should be at least aware or fear whenever you’ve done something that

includes Police to respond.

  • Give other players time to recover from the previous engagement before they

were sent to the hospital for treatment (Hospital Treatment Scenarios).

  • Players should not be inserting themselves into ongoing situations or shootouts,

between two groups or one group and the police. (Intercept/Gang Rules)

  • Forcing yourself into roleplay (civilian, gang or police) scenarios.
  • If you are asked to leave, you are to do so.

Just act differently. Be creative on your character’s NLR situation.

Injury & Recovery Protocol

  • You can NOT talk back while you are unconscious.
  • You are injured for 10-30 minutes depending on the injuries.
  • You cannot immediately run after someone for revenge.
  • You’re not allowed to go back to the area you’ve been downed for an hour or when

there’s no active scenario.

For example, if you are stabbed multiple times in the legs and you just got patched up, there is no way you can run normally.

  • Always try to state your character’s condition by using /me
  • When EMS uses /me checks wounds - you are supposed to reply using /me has

Stabbed wounds. (Or whatever your current situation is)

Ex. /me Heavy bleeding from a gunshot wound in the body.

“My leg still hurts!/Ang sakit pa ng binti ko!” than

“Recovery pa ko eh” (with a tone of not feeling injured)

  • You are NOT ALLOWED to trash talk an unconscious player. Do not do this. Here is why:

Elaborated Version

  • 1. Assume responders are coming

If police/medic responders are part of the gameplay or roleplay, trash talking an unconscious player can disrupt the scenario. It breaks immersion and can interfere with how responders are supposed to handle the situation.

  • 2. It can create toxicity

When someone is already down and unable to respond, trash talking becomes one-sided. That often escalates negativity and can make the community feel hostile instead of healthy competitive.

  • 3. If you’re going to trash talk, do it when they can respond

Competitive banter usually works best when both players are active participants. When someone is conscious and playing, it stays more like normal rivalry rather than targeting someone who can’t interact.

Conflict & Criminal Conduct

Standards for escalation, criminal scenes, emergency services, crew limits, and restart protection.

Violence & Scene Initiation

  • Random Death Match (RDM)

Attacking/killing other players/groups without any roleplay prior to it.

  • Vehicle DeathMatch (VDM)

Intentionally killing another player/group using a vehicle without any roleplay leading up to it. Situational/Case to case basis: if a player is in a tight area and someone fires a gun on him, he can (unintentionally) run over a player. (must provide proof)

other party first (NO ONE-LINERS), but if the situation calls for it let there be a Warning shot then wait for the other party to acknowledge the gunplay.

Ex. ““Taas kamay!”” then immediate gunfire - X

demands. (This involves kidnapping or holdup scenarios)

  • Do not take items that are of no use for your rp.
  • Avoid dumping a vehicle in the ocean without any proper rp/valid reason to do so.

Organization Conflict — General Standards

  • Gang Wars should have an RP or a story behind that led up to the war
  • Petty gang war reasons will fall into Fail RP/Forced RP.
