General

Defaults that apply everywhere, on foot or off duty.

TOpen the chat input box.
F2Open your player inventory.
KOpen a nearby or targeted secondary inventory (stash, trunk, etc.).
RReload your currently held weapon.
TABShow or hide the item hotbar.
1 – 5Use the item in that hotbar slot.
= (Equals)Open the radio menu.
. / ,Switch to the next or previous radio channel.
F11Cycle through voice proximity distance modes.
Left AltHold to transmit over your tuned radio channel.
Gamepad D-pad UpAlternate push-to-talk bind.
Left AltToggle the targeting/interaction mode.
ZOpen or close the radial interaction menu.
XCancel your active progress bar.
KToggle crouching on foot.
XRaise or lower your hands. Arrest/robbery scenes.
HOMEShow nearby players' server IDs. Useful for reports.
ETackle a nearby fleeing suspect.
BackspaceClose the binoculars view.
XStand up from a "sit anywhere" seated position.
XGet out of the hospital recovery bed early.
XStand up out of the train-seat animation.
EInteract with casino games.

Roleplay

Emotes and character animation.

F3Open the animation/emote menu.
BackspaceStop your current animation.
XOpen your favourites animation radial wheel. Hold.

Vehicle

Driving, seating, and vehicle controls.

BBuckle or unbuckle your seatbelt.
YToggle cruise control while driving.
E / Shift+EPush a nearby vehicle. Hold Shift to push harder.
F9Toggle the in-vehicle radio on or off.
GTurn the vehicle engine on or off.
LLock or unlock your vehicle.
OShuffle to another seat in a vehicle.

Phone

The one keybind for your in-game phone.

MOpen your phone.

Property

Getting around and managing property.

EInteract with a nearby elevator.

Police

On-duty police and sheriff controls.

F6Open the police interaction menu (cuff, search, drag).
F7Toggle the in-vehicle speed radar.
[ (OEM_4)Lock the radar's front antenna on a target.
] (OEM_6)Lock the radar's rear antenna on a target.
\ (OEM_5)Cycle the radar's direction filter.
EStop carrying a restrained or dragged person.
GRappel from a helicopter. Requires a rappel kit item.
GToggle the on-screen radio channel member list. Police/sheriff only.
LToggle the vehicle siren control HUD. Emergency vehicles only.
JToggle emergency lights.
BCycle siren tone or arm the siren.
` (OEM_3)Mute or unmute the siren audio.
XManual horn / hi-lo burst.
F9Open the dispatch/alerts panel.
ZAccept a dispatch alert.
ODismiss a dispatch alert.
GView a dispatch alert.
JExpand a dispatch alert.

EMS

On-duty EMS and medical controls.

F5Open the EMS dispatch/alerts menu.
DELETEOpen the medic dispatch/reports board.
F6Open the EMS medical assessment view of nearby patients.

Other

Controls for a specific minigame — all while seated at a poker table.

EnterReady up.
VPeek at your cards.
SpaceFocus the table view.
~ (Tilde)Toggle the cursor.
HToggle the legend/help overlay.
TabToggle the UI panel.
FFold.
CCall.
RRaise.
1 – 5Table emotes.
Y / U / I / OIdle animations.
Same key, different job

A few keys are reused on purpose — for example E and L do different things depending on whether you're on foot, driving, or in a police vehicle or helicopter. Job-restricted keybinds only work while you hold that job.
