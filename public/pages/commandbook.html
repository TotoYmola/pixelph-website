General

Chat, voice, and everyday utility commands available to every player.

/clearchat

Clear your chat window.

/pm [id] [message]

Send a private message to another player.

/ui_target

Open the interaction menu's theme picker.

/jammer

Open the radio jammer setup menu.

/rechargeradio

Recharge your radio's battery.

/remradiodata

Remove your saved radio data.

/setvoiceintent [speech|music]

Set your mic's audio processing mode (noise suppression on or off).

/vol [1-100]

Set your radio and phone playback volume.

/cycleproximity

Cycle through voice proximity distance modes.

/removemask

Remove your equipped medical mask.

/removevest

Remove your currently worn body armor/vest.

/removeprops

Remove props currently stuck or attached to your character.

/cancelprogress

Cancel your active progress bar.

/ox_lib

Open your settings menu for notification sound and position.

/divingsuithud

Reposition the diving suit's oxygen HUD.

/removegasmask

Remove your equipped gas mask.

/removenightvision

Remove your equipped night vision goggles.

/togglenightvision

Toggle night vision mode while wearing NVGs.

/togglethermalvision

Toggle thermal vision mode while wearing NVGs.

/group

Open your group/party menu (shared jobs, heists, dispatch).

Character

Check your own character, job, and standing.

/id

Check your own server player ID.

/job

Check your current job, grade, and duty status.

/gang

Check your current gang and grade.

/xpdisplay

Open the XP/skill progress display menu.

/health

Open your personal health record (illnesses/vitals).

/gymstats

View your own gym training stats.

Roleplay

Tools for playing your character — actions, emotes, and expression.

/ooc [message]

Send an out-of-character message to nearby players.

/me [message]

Show a roleplay action message above your character.

/steal

Open a nearby player's inventory to pickpocket them.

/animations

Open the animation and emote menu.

/scene [text]

Place a decorative roleplay text/prop scene at your location.

/hidescenes

Toggle visibility of placed scenes for yourself.

Vehicle

Commands for driving, trunks, and vehicle ownership.

/getintrunk

Climb into a nearby vehicle's trunk.

/putintrunk

Put a nearby target player into a vehicle trunk.

/hotwire

Start hotwiring a vehicle you don't have keys for.

/myfinance

View/manage your financed vehicles.

/directsale

Open the direct sale tablet (inside a dealership).

/calltow

Request a tow truck.

Jobs

Commands tied to a specific job.

/ctm

Cancel your current trucking delivery mission.

Business

Commands tied to running a business.

/cmechanic

Open the mechanic shop creator to set up a new shop. Real estate job only.

/setmechanicowner

Set or transfer a mechanic shop's owner. Real estate job only.

/locksmith

Open the Locksmith business actions menu. Locksmith business members only.

Mechanic

Commands for the mechanic job.

/towdispatch

Open the tow-call dispatch queue.

Police

Commands for on-duty police and sheriff officers.

/callsign [callsign]

Set your police unit callsign.

/issueTicket

Open the traffic ticket menu to fine or cite a nearby player.

/radarconfig

Reposition the in-vehicle speed radar display.

/radiolist

Reposition the radio channel member list display.

/mdt

Open the Mobile Data Terminal.

/panic

Panic button — send a high-priority alert with your location to dispatch.

EMS

Commands for on-duty EMS and medical staff.

/911 [message]

Send a 911 emergency alert with your location.

/bed

Admit the nearest player into a hospital recovery bed.

/hospital, /patients

Open the EMS hospital dashboard (bed status & patients).

Other

Everything that didn't fit a category above.

/record

Start a Rockstar Editor recording.

/clip

Mark a clip point while recording.

/saveclip

Save your Rockstar Editor clip.

/delclip

Discard your Rockstar Editor clip.

/editor

Open the Rockstar Editor.

/report

Send a report to online staff.

/close_ticket

Close the train-ticket viewer window.

/trainticket

Re-show your most recently issued train ticket.

Job-restricted commands

Commands under Police, EMS, Mechanic, Business, and Jobs only work while you hold that job (and, for some, while on duty). They won't do anything otherwise.
