PixelPH Rulebook

The sections below define the core standard expected from every whitelisted player. Where a situation is not covered word-for-word, use good faith and protect the roleplay.

1. Roleplay & Immersion

Remain in character during active situations. Avoid intentionally breaking immersion, using out-of-character information in character, or treating serious scenes like disposable minigames.

2. Metagaming & Stream Sniping

Information learned outside your character's legitimate experience cannot be used in roleplay. This includes streams, Discord, chats, clips, or information from another character.

3. Powergaming & Forced RP

Do not use mechanics or roleplay actions to force unreasonable outcomes on another player. Give others a meaningful chance to react and keep actions within believable limits.

4. Exploits, Cheats & Unfair Advantages

Cheats, exploits, abusive macros, injected tools, and unfair modifications are prohibited. If you discover a bug with economic or gameplay impact, report it and stop using it.

5. New Life & Memory

After a character is legitimately incapacitated and the scene concludes, do not immediately return to continue the same conflict. Treat serious injury and memory consistently with the situation and staff guidance.

6. Criminal Roleplay

Criminal activity must create roleplay, not only profit. Escalation should have context, and players are expected to respect scenario limits, cooldowns, and police interaction rules announced by staff.

7. Police, EMS & Government Roles

Whitelisted public-service roles carry a higher standard. Abuse of access, corruption outside approved storylines, or using job information for alternate characters is not allowed.

8. Community Conduct

Harassment, hate speech, real-life threats, doxxing, targeted discrimination, and deliberate attempts to drive players out of the community are prohibited.

9. Staff Cases & Evidence

Use the proper support process for reports and appeals. Do not turn in-character conflicts into out-of-character harassment. Staff may request clips or context when reviewing a case.

10. Common-Sense Clause

No rulebook can describe every possible situation. Attempts to exploit wording or technical loopholes can still be actioned when they clearly violate the intent of serious roleplay or fair play.

Final enforcement details, numerical limits, and department-specific policies may be published in Discord. Check announcements before playing.
